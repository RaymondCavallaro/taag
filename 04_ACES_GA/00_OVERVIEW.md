# ACES-GA Overview

Status: experimental

ACES-GA is the advanced geometric/formal substrate for ACES.

This layer is intentionally isolated from the operational TAAG manual. It may become important for simulation, formal analysis, and deep theory, but it should not be required for first practical use.

Core idea:

Adaptive systems can be represented as multivector-like structures whose scalar, vector, bivector, and higher-grade components encode viability intensity, directed flows, tensions, tradeoffs, and multiscale coordination regimes.
