# Experimental

Status: experimental

This directory contains speculative, incomplete, or risky ideas that are not part of the current core framework.

Experimental material should be easy to inspect without being mistaken for canonical ACES/TAAG.

## Experimental Areas

- `historical_interpretations/`: speculative structural readings of historical concepts, institutions, or practices through ACES/TAAG.
- `ai_conditioning/`: experimental AI reasoning scaffolds, critique protocols, and conditioning hypotheses.
- `personal_adaptive_practices/`: experimental everyday-life heuristics interpreted through ACES/TAAG, with extra caution for health, behavior, and wellbeing domains.
- `protocols/`: experimental rules for how exploratory material should be labeled, tested, challenged, and revised.

## Required Metadata

Experimental files should identify:

- status,
- epistemic class,
- confidence,
- purpose,
- what the document does not claim,
- validation pathway,
- and exit paths.

## Core Rule

Experimental material may generate future pathways, but it should not silently become canon.
