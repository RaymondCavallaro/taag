# Pseudo-Agent Stabilization

## Status

[EXPLORATORY]

Some adaptive systems may exhibit:

* directional behavior,
* coordination patterns,
* pressure responses,
* persistence dynamics,
* and apparent strategic continuity

without possessing:

* unified cognition,
* centralized agency,
* or coherent intentional identity.

Examples may include:

* institutions,
* markets,
* crowds,
* synchronized networks,
* bureaucratic structures,
* and distributed algorithmic systems.

---

# Important Constraint

Pseudo-agents should NOT automatically be interpreted as:

* fully coherent agents,
* unified minds,
* singular intentional entities,
* or fully centralized decision structures.

Apparent continuity may emerge from:

* topology,
* synchronization,
* pressure routing,
* institutional inertia,
* and distributed adaptive reinforcement.

---

# Stabilization Goal

ACES should preserve:

# bounded pseudo-agent interpretation

without collapsing into:

* naive anthropomorphism,
* total reductionism,
* or unified-agent projection.
