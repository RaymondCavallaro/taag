# AI Assistance Notice

Status: draft

Portions of this framework, documentation, structural refinement, and exploratory reasoning were developed with the assistance of large language models, primarily ChatGPT.

The repository treats AI systems as reasoning participants rather than authoritative sources. All concepts, structures, and conclusions remain subject to revision, critique, operational testing, and grounded validation.

AI assistance has been used primarily for:

- structural refinement,
- terminology exploration,
- summarization,
- adversarial critique,
- document organization,
- and reasoning scaffolding.

Final responsibility for direction, inclusion, interpretation, and release governance remains with the human maintainers.

## Why This Is Disclosed

This notice preserves:

- transparency,
- grounded observability,
- anti-mythology,
- authorship clarity,
- AI-context integrity,
- and anti-self-reference discipline.

ACES/TAAG explicitly studies AI interaction patterns, reasoning scaffolds, observability, and adaptive governance. Hidden AI assistance would be less consistent with the framework than visible, bounded disclosure.

## How To Interpret AI-Assisted Material

AI-assisted material should be evaluated by the same standards as all other material:

- Does it improve clarity?
- Does it preserve uncertainty?
- Does it remain grounded?
- Does it expose assumptions?
- Does it improve diagnosis, explanation, or action?
- Does it remain open to critique and revision?

AI fluency is not evidence of correctness. AI involvement does not make a claim authoritative, and it does not make a claim invalid by itself.
