# Known Failures

Status: draft

Known failure modes should be documented openly so the framework remains analyzable.

## Framework-Level Failure Modes

- Concept inflation: adding terms faster than they can be tested.
- Premature formalization: making the math central before the operational layer is stable.
- Monolithic drift: editing a giant compiled document instead of modular sources.
- AI overconfidence: treating model outputs as authoritative diagnoses.
- Case study thinness: applying concepts without grounded detail.
- Self-reference capture: using ACES/TAAG to legitimize itself without challenge.

## Analysis-Level Failure Modes

- Confusing popularity with legitimacy.
- Confusing efficiency with adaptive viability.
- Treating metrics as reality.
- Ignoring missing participants.
- Underestimating switching costs and dependency lock-in.
- Recommending irreversible interventions from weak evidence.
