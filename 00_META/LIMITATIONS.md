# Limitations

Status: draft

ACES/TAAG is a reasoning framework, not an oracle.

It can help structure analysis, reveal missing signals, identify capture dynamics, and compare interventions, but it does not automatically prove what is true or what should be done.

## Core Limitations

- It depends on the quality and grounding of available information.
- It can be misused to create an illusion of rigor.
- It may over-abstract lived experience if examples and consequences are not preserved.
- It may underperform in urgent situations requiring domain experts, legal authority, medical expertise, or direct operational control.
- It can become self-referential if the framework is treated as unquestionable.

## Required Caution

Every ACES/TAAG analysis should identify:

- what is observed,
- what is inferred,
- what is unknown,
- who is affected,
- which metrics may be distorted,
- what decisions are irreversible,
- and what should not be concluded from the analysis.
