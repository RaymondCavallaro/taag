# Cross-Discipline Synchronization (Draft)

ACES does not attempt to replace specialized disciplines.

Instead:
ACES increasingly acts as:
- an interoperability,
- synchronization,
- and adaptive propagation coordination layer.

Different disciplines stabilize:
- different scopes,
- adaptive units,
- abstractions,
- metrics,
- and continuity concerns.

Examples:

| Discipline | Typical Scope |
|---|---|
| epidemiology | transmission propagation |
| medicine | organism continuity |
| ecology | ecosystem interaction |
| economics | allocation dynamics |
| psychology | cognition and behavior |
| governance | institutional coordination |
| engineering | bounded operational systems |

ACES may synchronize these through shared adaptive structures such as:
- pressure,
- topology,
- conductance,
- reconstructability,
- continuity,
- optionality,
- propagation,
- coordination,
- and adaptive debt.

The goal is not:
- universal ontology collapse,
but:
- bounded interoperability and reconstructable coordination.
