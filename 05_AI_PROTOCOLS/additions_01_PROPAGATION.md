# Propagation Protocols (Draft)

ACES increasingly treats adaptive propagation as:
- topology-conditioned,
- mechanism-dependent,
- and bounded by reconstructability constraints.

Variable changes alone do not automatically justify:
- causal certainty,
- broad narrative conclusions,
- or unrestricted propagation assumptions.

Instead, propagation analysis increasingly specifies:

| Component | Purpose |
|---|---|
| source variable | what changed |
| target variable | what may change |
| propagation mechanism | how propagation occurs |
| topology conditions | required structural conditions |
| buffering conditions | what weakens propagation |
| confidence | propagation confidence |
| delay | expected timescale |
| reversibility | recovery possibility |
| evidence basis | reconstruction support |

Example:

Instead of:

clinic overload causes trust collapse

ACES increasingly prefers:

Under:
- visible healthcare failure,
- repeated delays,
- and high institutional dependence,

healthcare overload may propagate pressure toward legitimacy degradation
through:
- perceived unreliability,
- suffering visibility,
- and expectation mismatch.

This preserves:

bounded inference,
inspectability,
and reconstructability.
