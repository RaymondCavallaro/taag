# Cross-Domain Pressure Routing

ACES increasingly models pressure as propagating across:
- disciplinary boundaries,
- institutional layers,
- cognitive systems,
- infrastructures,
- economies,
- and ecological environments.

Examples:

- environmental pressure may propagate into healthcare burden,
- healthcare burden may propagate into economic strain,
- economic strain may propagate into legitimacy degradation,
- legitimacy degradation may propagate into coordination failure.

ACES therefore increasingly treats pressure-routing analysis as:
- multi-layer,
- topology-aware,
- and cross-domain interoperable.

One important operational role of ACES may become:
# adaptive signal routing between specialized analyses.

Meaning:

When variable X changes in one analysis layer,
ACES may help identify:
- which other analysis layers should update,
- through what propagation mechanisms,
- and under what conditions.
