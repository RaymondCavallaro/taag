# Reconstructive Disagreement

## Status

[STRONG EMERGING]

Disagreement should not automatically be treated as:

* irrationality,
* hostility,
* ignorance,
* or adversarial failure.

Different agents may preserve access to:

* different signals,
* different topology visibility,
* different reconstruction paths,
* different adaptive pressures,
* and different observability limitations.

---

# Protocol Objective

The objective is NOT:

# forced consensus

but:

# reconstructable disagreement analysis.

Healthy systems attempt to identify:

* missing signals,
* topology asymmetries,
* interpretive divergence,
* pressure distortions,
* framing conflicts,
* and observability gaps.

---

# Important Constraint

Agreement alone should NOT be treated as:

* truth,
* legitimacy,
* reconstructability,
* or adaptive health.

Systems may achieve:

* synchronized distortion,
* coordinated blindness,
* or topology-enforced convergence.

---

# Healthy Disagreement

Healthy disagreement preserves:

* analyzability,
* bounded interpretability,
* uncertainty visibility,
* layered reasoning,
* and reconstructable divergence paths.
