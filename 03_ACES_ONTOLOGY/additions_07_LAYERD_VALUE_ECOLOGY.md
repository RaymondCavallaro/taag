# Layered Value Ecology

## Status

[EXPLORATORY]

Value systems may emerge from:

* cognition,
* identity,
* topology,
* interaction ecology,
* memory,
* participation structure,
* and adaptive continuity pressures.

Values should not necessarily be treated as:

* purely abstract ideals,
* fixed primitives,
* or isolated moral objects.

Instead values may behave as:

# emergent adaptive orientation structures.

---

# Important Constraint

Layered value ecology should NOT collapse into:

* pure utility optimization,
* pure emotional weighting,
* pure ideology,
* pure continuity preservation,
* or pure social conformity.

Different adaptive layers may produce:

* partially conflicting,
* temporally divergent,
* or topology-dependent

value structures simultaneously.

---

# Observability Constraint

Value systems may become:

* symbolic,
* ritualized,
* institutionalized,
* compressed,
* fragmented,
* or externally imposed

while partially disconnected from:

* adaptive reality,
* reconstructability,
* or participation conditions.

Healthy systems preserve:

# reconstructable value interaction
across adaptive layers.
